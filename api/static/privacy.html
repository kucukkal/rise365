Rise365 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Rise365 is a faith-based mobile app for tracking daily prayers, tasks, and spiritual growth. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect: phone number, name, prayer and task completion records, device token for push notifications.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to manage your account, send SMS verification, track progress, send notifications, and calculate achievements.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share only with Twilio, Firebase, Cloudflare, and Railway for app functionality.

5. Data Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. We use SMS OTP authentication with no passwords stored.

6. Data Retention

Data is retained while your account is active. Request deletion at privacy@rise365app.com within 30 days.

7. Push Notifications

Rise365 sends prayer, task, and event reminders. Disable anytime in iPhone Settings.

8. Children Privacy

Rise365 is not for children under 13. We do not collect data from children under 13.

9. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your data by contacting privacy@rise365app.com.
